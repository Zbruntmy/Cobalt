# COBALT app: setup (one time, about 15 minutes, easiest from a PC)

## 0. Save your progress first
Open your current COBALT → **Export progress** → Copy. Paste it somewhere safe (a note or an email to yourself).

## 1. Put COBALT online (GitHub Pages, free)
1. Go to github.com → sign in → **New repository**.
2. Name it `cobalt`. Set it to **Public** (free Pages hosting needs a public repo). Click **Create repository**.
3. Click **"uploading an existing file"**. Drag in EVERYTHING from this zip, including the `icons` folder. Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment*, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
5. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your app lives at **https://Zbruntmy.github.io/Cobalt/**

## 2. Install it on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap **⬇ Install app** on the home screen (or ⋮ menu → *Install app*).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

It opens full-screen with the COBALT icon and works offline.

## 3. Bring your progress back
Open the installed app → **Import progress** → paste → Import.

## Updating later
When Claude sends a new build, upload the new `index.html` and `sw.js` to the repo (they replace the old ones). The app picks up the update the next time it opens with internet. Your progress stays put.

## Notes
- A public repo means anyone with the link can open your deck. Your progress is NOT in the repo; it only lives on your devices.
- Progress is per device. Use Export / Import to move it between phone and PC.
